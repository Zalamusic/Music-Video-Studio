# CutLoopz My Studio — Photo & Readability Refinement

- Moved the single standard artist photo selector from Page 3 (Director) to Page 1 (Your Song & Artist). The same DOM ID, validation, and draft filename mechanism remain in place.
- Added a local, in-browser image preview, released on replacement/reset/navigation. No images are uploaded or saved by this preview.
- Raised small mobile labels, supporting copy, navigation, scene editor, and draft status text for readability while preserving the compact step layout.
- Photo filename can survive drafts, but the photo file itself must be reselected after reload. Glamour Membership photo/wardrobe options remain separate.
- No payments, GPU generation, private admin routes, Render deployment, or official domain were changed.
