# CutLoopz — Home shortcut and full lyrics board

- Added a fixed, compact, accessible Home house icon at the bottom left of every customer-facing page, opposite the existing Back to Top arrow.
- The Home shortcut takes visitors to `/customer.html#home`; it does not create a new project or erase the Studio draft.
- On My Studio Page 1, lyrics grow to the full pasted/restored content (up to the existing 12,000-character limit), instead of showing only the first few lines in a nested scrolling area.
- The board recalculates after typing, paste, resize, restoring draft, and Back/Edit Song navigation.
- The standalone Director Preview lyrics field also auto-expands.
- Existing Save, Storyboard Download, project backups, audio rules, pricing, and GPU limits are unchanged.
- Only user-interface files were modified; no paid or GPU operations occur.
