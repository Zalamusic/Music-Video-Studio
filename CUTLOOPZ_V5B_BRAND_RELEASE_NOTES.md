# CutLoopz V5B — SUNO + No Credits / Just Video

This release supersedes V5 (55 tests) with V5B (57 tests). Upload the **V5B** YAML first to `.github/workflows/`, then upload the **V5B** release ZIP to the repository root. Do not upload the older V5 files.

## Visible changes
- SUNO is uppercase and boldly presented on the customer homepage and My Studio; other provider names are also bold.
- Prominent “NO CREDITS. JUST VIDEO.” explanation: planned per-finished-video pricing, with price, deliverables and revision terms disclosed before checkout.
- Preview ordering and payments remain disabled. The tagline is the planned customer-facing commercial model, not a claim that video generation already works.
- SUNO provider confirmation messages are uppercase.
- Existing song link handling, free Analyzer, Listening Director, protected production services, and draft saving are preserved.

## Limitations
- Pasting a song link saves it for creative planning; it does not extract or process audio directly. Authorized MP3/WAV upload is needed for audio analysis.
- No changes to GPU endpoints, payment services, or official domain.
